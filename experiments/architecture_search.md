# Architecture Search

The final GW-Mamba model was obtained after exploring multiple model families over approximately 154 experimental iterations.

## Architecture Families

- LSTM / GRU
- CNN-based sequence models
- Temporal Convolution Networks (TCN)
- Graph Convolution Networks (GCN)
- Graph Attention Networks (GAT)
- MD-GRTN
- Graph WaveNet
- Physics-informed architectures
- Graph WaveNet + Mamba (Final)

Each family introduced new architectural ideas, training strategies, or preprocessing techniques that were evaluated before converging on the final design.

For detailed discussion, refer to the Research Report.
